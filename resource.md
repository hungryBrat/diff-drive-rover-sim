- Articulated Robotics (Josh Newans, YouTube) — the channel for this exact project. His mobile-robot series covers ros2_control, Nav2, and Gazebo integration almost 1:1 with what you're building. Start here.
- Nav2 docs (docs.nav2.org) — the official tutorials are genuinely good; do them, don't just skim.
- Robotics Back-End (Edouard Renard, roboticsbackend.com) — clean ROS2 node/action/param explanations for when you write your waypoint node.


### Theory for the EKF (gate these in front of phase 6):

- Cyrill Stachniss (YouTube, Uni Bonn) — his Kalman filter and SLAM lectures are the best free ones anywhere. Watch the KF/EKF ones before you write the filter.
- "Kalman and Bayesian Filters in Python" by Roger Labbe — free interactive Jupyter book. This is the hands-on way to actually get EKF, not just watch it. Perfect for your "rusty but willing" math.
- Steve Brunton (YouTube) — short, intuition-first Kalman/control videos when you want the why in 15 min.


### Foundations for the long game (project #2 onward):

- "Modern Robotics" (Lynch & Park) — free PDF + Coursera + YouTube. Kinematics/dynamics done right.
- "Probabilistic Robotics" (Thrun, Burgard, Fox) — the bible for estimation/SLAM. Reference it, don't read cover-to-cover.
